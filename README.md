# FallSeed · Creator-OS

> **The level-2 seed.** A sovereign single-HTML PWA for managing a personal operating system around a chosen persona. 6 persona presets: Creator · Investor · Researcher · Writer · Nomad · Monk.

**Live:** https://sjgant80-hub.github.io/fallseed-creator-os/

## What this is

`fallseed-creator-os` is one HTML file. Pick a persona; the entire bundle (goal templates, routine templates, journal prompts, knowledge categories, agent tasks, dashboard widgets) swaps to match. Track your own life on your own device.

## Personas shipped

- **◉ Creator** — make, ship, refine. Output is the proof.
- **◇ Investor** — capital flows, positions, conviction.
- **◈ Researcher** — deep reading, careful synthesis, traceable claims.
- **◍ Writer** — words shipped, voice developed, audience grown.
- **◐ Nomad** — light footprint, work anywhere, optimise the admin.
- **◌ Monk** — less, slower, deeper.

Add custom personas via the Build tab (cascade-driven).

## What kind of seed is this?

A **level-2** seed in the FallSeed family. A level-0 seed packages one industry's tools. A level-1 seed packages a class of verticals. A level-2 seed packages a class of *decisions* — the personas above are six pre-loaded decision-substrates. Built on the Fork Seed primitive — see [the spec](https://www.ai-nativesolutions.com/spec.html) for the four invariants of replication, the SEED schema, and the six-step fork protocol.
